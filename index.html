About Highway Rush

Highway Rush is a free endless racing game. Steer between lanes, weave through traffic, collect coins and push your speed higher with every second. How far can you go?

How to play

  • Press Play and steer your red car along the four-lane highway.
  • Avoid other vehicles. One hit ends the run.
  • Collect gold coins for 10 bonus points each.
  • Pass close to a car without touching it for a 50 point near-miss bonus.
  • Speed rises over time, and traffic gets denser.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Left/Right arrows or A/D to steer. P or Esc pauses.
  • Touch: hold the large ◀ and ▶ buttons at the bottom of the game.
